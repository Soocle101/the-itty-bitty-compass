Factual Polling & Scoring Methodology

1. Forced-Choice + 2x Issue Priority Weighting Every item presents three steel-manned policy stances written in plain language. You can toggle ★ High Priority (2x Weight) on issues that matter most to your vote.
2. Pew Research & Cross-Pressure Calibration Your US Spectrum placement is benchmarked against the empirical median American voter, mapped to Pew Typology clusters, and analyzed for ideological cross-pressure.
3. Congressional & International Party Data US caucus placements reflect DW-NOMINATE congressional roll-call patterns, while international party placements are calibrated to Chapel Hill Expert Survey (CHES) coordinates.
4. Policy Divergence & Heterodox Detection No voter agrees with their party 100% of the time. The results dashboard pinpoints the exact policy topics where your answers broke ranks with your #1 matched ideology or caucus.
